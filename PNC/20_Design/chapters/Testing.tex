\subsubsection{\textit{Port Interface : PNC_ActiveTxPDUEnable}}

	\item The port PP_Active_TxPDU_Enable_ComMPnc_Propulsion is connected to the SUM_SSC, SUM_SOH, and SUM_SSFM-ERRH. The port will send
